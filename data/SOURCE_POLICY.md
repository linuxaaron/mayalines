# Quote data policy

`quotes.json` contains only records that have explicit provenance metadata.

A record may be published as indexable only when:

- the exact quotation wording is matched to a concrete source,
- the author attribution is verified,
- the copyright status for the target jurisdiction is cleared,
- and the exact source URL is stored.

Discovery datasets can be used to identify candidates, but a dataset license does not automatically clear the underlying quotation text.

For source research, see `docs/QUOTE_SOURCES.md`.
