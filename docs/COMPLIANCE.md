# Compliance and launch checklist

This checklist is an engineering baseline, not legal advice. Legal applicability depends on the operator, business model, target markets and third-party services actually used.

## Launch blockers

- [ ] Replace all `[ ... ]` legal placeholders in `/imprint`, `/privacy` and `/accessibility`.
- [ ] Confirm the legal publisher/controller and a valid postal address.
- [ ] Confirm contact and accessibility-reporting email addresses.
- [ ] Confirm the final domain and set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in Vercel.
- [ ] Review the final hosting configuration and document relevant technical logs/data processing.
- [ ] Before advertising/analytics: document every provider, purpose, legal basis, recipient, transfer, retention and consent requirement.
- [ ] Before personalized Google advertising: configure a Google-certified CMP integrated with IAB TCF where required by Google policy.
- [ ] Verify quotation rights, attribution and source evidence for every indexable quote.
- [ ] Perform manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast and mobile tests.
- [ ] Run an accessibility audit against the accessibility standard applicable to the actual service and operator.

## Current engineering baseline

- Semantic landmarks and headings.
- Keyboard-visible focus states.
- Skip navigation link.
- Accessible labels for search and copy controls.
- `aria-live` feedback for search results.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Responsive layouts for small screens.
- `robots.txt` and sitemap generated by Next.js.
- Canonical metadata for indexable content.
- BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on quote/category pages.
- Non-indexable quote records remain excluded from the sitemap.
- Author pages remain `noindex` until they have sufficient original editorial value and verified biographical/source information.

## Advertising/privacy rule

Do not add Google AdSense, analytics, tracking pixels or other non-essential third-party scripts until the privacy policy and consent implementation match the actual services. For EEA, UK and Swiss personalized advertising, Google requires a certified CMP integrated with IAB TCF under its EU User Consent Policy.

## International scope

There is no single implementation that automatically guarantees legal compliance in every country. The site should be treated as compliant only for jurisdictions and services that have been specifically reviewed for the final business model.
